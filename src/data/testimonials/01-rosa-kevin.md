Magical! We had a lovely 3-night stay. The hut is cosy and fully equipped. The mattress is very comfortable. There's a cute outdoor shelter with a great BBQ, perfect for dining al fresco. Millie is in a beautiful and peaceful location. It's perfect for a switch-off break. There's a quiet stream at the back of the hut, perfect to dip your toes in on a hot day. Paul and Glenys were welcoming and helpful, and they have plenty of recommendations on places to visit and local walks. We loved our stay and would definitely come back.
