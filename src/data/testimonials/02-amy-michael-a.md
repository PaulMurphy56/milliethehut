Our best Herefordshire holiday by far and definitely will be visiting again.
Husband is happy with all the wild walks and Butty Bach and I'm happy with the amazing views, the lazy river, the old Railway line, the vintage shops and the food!

Got to mention the amazing Millie. She's a beaut! Dangerously comfy bed, fabulous outdoor spaces for all weather, comfy deck chairs, plenty of wood to keep us going and a stunning location! Would be interesting to visit in winter, the log burner in the hut looks very inviting.

Our hosts Paul & Glenys have been wonderful. The pubs in town are super friendly too, people stop to say hello and ask where we are from, it's a lovely little community.
